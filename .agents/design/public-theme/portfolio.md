# Portfolio Public Theme Profile

## Use When

Use this profile for personal, studio, agency, creative-work, or case-study-led public themes.

## Target User

Visitors should quickly understand who the person or team is, what work they produce, and how to inspect proof of quality.

## First Viewport

- Lead with identity, selected work, or a strong work category.
- Keep navigation short and visible: work, news or notes, contact/account paths.
- Show real project, reference, or gallery imagery when CMS data provides it.

## Content Hierarchy

- Prioritize `mod_reference`, `mod_content`, and `mod_page surfaces.`
- News and publication blocks should support credibility without overpowering work samples.
- Keep `mod_news`, `mod_publication`, `mod_video_embed`, `mod_imagecategories`, and search available when the baseline exposes them; treat them as notes, proof, or media support rather than replacing the work path.
- If a homepage module moves below the primary work stack, document the reason in `templates/<theme>/DESIGN.md`.
- Detail pages should give media, title, summary, body content, and related links a clear sequence.

## Header And Footer

- Header may be compact and identity-led, but must preserve search, language switching, account actions, and nested menu behavior when present.
- Footer should expose contact, menu branches, and supporting site metadata without becoming a large marketing panel.

## Card And Detail Rhythm

- Use consistent project/work cards with stable media ratios.
- Keep case-study detail pages calmer than the homepage.
- Allow one high-emphasis featured item, then use denser supporting grids or lists.

## Imagery

- Prefer real work, client, or gallery images.
- Avoid abstract decorative assets when CMS media can identify the work.
- Keep crops inspectable on mobile.

## Motion

- Use restrained reveal or hover motion only when it improves orientation.
- Do not hide essential content behind animation.

## Mobile Behavior

- Put identity, primary work, and first action inside the early scroll.
- Keep cards easy to tap with clear media and title boundaries.
- Nested navigation must stay reachable inside the mobile menu.
