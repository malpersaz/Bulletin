# Content-Heavy Public Theme Profile

## Use When

Use this profile for publication, news, archive, media, category-heavy, or broad CMS sites.

## Target User

Visitors should quickly scan many content options, filter by interest, and move into detail pages without losing orientation.

## First Viewport

- Lead with current or featured content and a clear route into categories or search.
- Avoid a single oversized hero that hides the content inventory.
- Make the second content band visible early.

## Content Hierarchy

- Prioritize `mod_news`, `mod_content`, `mod_publication`, `mod_video_embed`, `mod_imagecategories`, and search.
- Keep `mod_reference`, `mod_client`, and `mod_page` available when the baseline exposes them; present them as compact directories, proof strips, or cross-links instead of dropping their surfaces.
- Use category, latest, featured, and archive rhythms deliberately.
- Do not let a single campaign hero or static marketing block hide the content inventory.

## Header And Footer

- Header should support section navigation, search, language switching, and nested menu discovery.
- Footer should expose major sections and supporting site metadata in a compact structure.

## Card And Detail Rhythm

- Use denser card grids, list rows, or mixed rails where content volume is high.
- Keep metadata consistent across modules.
- Detail pages should keep users oriented with related content, category paths, share controls, and pagination where present.

## Imagery

- Use images to identify content type and subject.
- Keep thumbnails stable and avoid crops that hide the subject on mobile.
- Video and gallery modules should be visually distinct without breaking the shared rhythm.

## Motion

- Avoid motion that shifts large lists while scanning.
- Use loading states and progressive reveal only when they preserve position and orientation.

## Mobile Behavior

- Keep search and section paths close to the top.
- Prefer compact vertical cards with stable image/title/metadata order.
- Make pagination or progressive loading easy to use by touch.
