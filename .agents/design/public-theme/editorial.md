# Editorial Public Theme Profile

## Use When

Use this profile for reading-heavy sites where articles, pages, publications, and long-form content dominate.

## Target User

Readers should find current stories, important sections, and detail pages without visual noise.

## First Viewport

- Lead with the strongest current article, page, publication, or curated content group.
- Make category, search, and language paths easy to find.
- Keep the first viewport content-led rather than campaign-led.

## Content Hierarchy

- Prioritize `mod_news`, `mod_publication`, `mod_content`, `mod_page`, and search surfaces.
- Use category and latest-content structures to show depth without overwhelming the first screen.
- Keep `mod_reference` and `mod_client` as compact resource or proof directories when the baseline exposes them; do not remove their list/detail coverage.
- Treat `mod_video_embed` and `mod_imagecategories` as story-support media unless the theme-local `DESIGN.md` documents a broader media-first direction.

## Header And Footer

- Header should support section scanning and nested navigation.
- Footer should expose menu branches, site metadata, and supporting links in a compact reading-oriented layout.

## Card And Detail Rhythm

- Use clear title, summary, date or metadata, and image order.
- Keep list cards scannable with consistent text density.
- Detail pages should optimize line length, vertical rhythm, image placement, share controls, and related content.

## Typography

- Body text readability is the primary design constraint.
- Use restrained heading scale and generous content spacing.
- Avoid display-heavy typography inside dense article cards.

## Imagery

- Images should support the article subject, not act as generic atmosphere.
- Maintain stable ratios in lists and allow full inspection on detail pages.

## Motion

- Keep motion minimal. Reading surfaces should not shift while users scan.

## Mobile Behavior

- Put article title, category or metadata, and first content path early.
- Keep lists dense enough for scanning while preserving touch targets.
- Search and language switching must remain easy to reach.
